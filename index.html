Keystep

A free piano coach that listens to your real piano. Put your phone on the music stand, press Start, and just play.

Before you start
  1. Turn your phone volume up so you can hear the coach.
  2. Stand the phone on the piano, close to the keys.
  3. When Safari asks to use the microphone, tap Allow.
